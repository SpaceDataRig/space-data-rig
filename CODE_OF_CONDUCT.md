
# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.
- Misrepresenting authorship, provenance, or the source of a measurement or
  result. This project is built on verifiable honesty; presenting a synthetic
  stand-in as a real observation, or fabricating contributors or citations, is a
  violation of this code.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of
acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.

## Enforcement scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Reporting a concern

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers through the contact channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (private advisory) or by direct message to a maintainer
on GitHub. All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and
kept confidential to the extent possible.

## Enforcement matrix

Project maintainers may follow these steps for behaviour they deem a violation:

1. **Correction** -- a private written warning and clarification of why the conduct
   was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** -- a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** -- a temporary ban from interaction in the project space.
4. **Permanent ban** -- a permanent ban from the project's public space.
