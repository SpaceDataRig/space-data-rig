Generated by `scripts/p106-centroid-roc.ts`. p104 refused to promote the pixel-centroid F-test because at
alpha=0.001 it caught 0 of 59 testable blends. p106 asks whether some OTHER threshold rescues it: it sweeps
the stored, threshold-free target p-values over 59 testable blends and 55 testable planets (real labelled data) and reports a Mann-Whitney AUC plus the Youden-optimal cut. Fully offline over a committed fixture.

Reproduce: `npm run p106:centroid-roc`

- AUC of the target p-value (blend vs planet): **0.479** -> none separation.
- Neighbour-consistency AUC: 0.669.
- At alpha=0.001 (p104's point): caught 0/59, wrongly rejected 2/55.
- Youden-optimal: alpha=6.49e-1, sensitivity 0.63, specificity 0.49 (J=0.12).

Note on precision: the checkpoint stores p-values to 2 significant figures, so AUC ties from rounding are
scored at 0.5; the AUC is approximate to that storage precision.
