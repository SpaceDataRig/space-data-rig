P35 independent cross-check: PDM vs box-fit BLS (real photometry)

Sample: first 40 TIC-bearing hosts (T<=12, P in [1,13] d) in deterministic catalog order (the SAME seeded sample as p33); 33 had usable public TESS-SPOC curves (<=2 sectors stitched), 7 coverage misses. PDM (Stellingwerf 1978, src/detect/pdm.ts) is a non-parametric period finder sharing NO machinery with the box-fit family (bls.ts / iterative.ts): it minimizes the within-bin variance of the phase fold, theta. Inject period 3 d; depths [300, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000] ppm; bins 12; oversample 10; match tol 2%; seed 7.

PDM-vs-BLS agreement (same period or harmonic)9/18 (50.0%)

Agreement is corroboration between two independent estimators; disagreement localizes where the box-fit family locks a peak PDM does not share (often a systematics or alias peak). PDM's global theta minimum on a real variable star is frequently the dominant rotation/activity period, so a low agreement rate is expected and is itself the honest finding (it is why transit pipelines use a detrended box-fit search, not raw PDM).
