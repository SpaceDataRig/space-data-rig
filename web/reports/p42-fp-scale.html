Honest reading

  1. The FP cut and the AUC gain are reported PER SECTOR and in aggregate: the question a pilot cannot answer

-- does this hold on independent fields -- is measured, not assumed.

  1. The AUC is the auditable headline: it says, at every threshold at once, that the coherent-systematics null

separates a real transit from an instrumental artifact better than the noise-only gate. A near-0.5 AUC would mean no discrimination; a lower-bound AUC here still understates the true gain because unconfirmed planets are counted as false positives.

  1. Boundary (physics, not code): after subtraction the residual FP is each star's own variability, which the

per-star noise already models -- the single-sector purity ceiling (p31/p33), closable only with more data power and physical vetting, not a sharper significance model.

Measure-only: nothing gated, anchored, claimed or deployed. Enabling this gate in production still requires a same-cadence ensemble caller, the p40 measure-before-gate cert, and explicit operator go-ahead.
