P49 formal blind test of the detector on real photometry

Reproduce: npm run p49:blind-test -- --stars 30 --maxsectors 2 --inject-frac 0.5 --seed 7

A seeded coin planted one synthetic planet (random period/depth from a documented prior, logged in a sealed answer key the detector never saw) on some real TESS-SPOC curves and left the rest null; the production gated detector then ran on every curve and its detections were scored against the key.
