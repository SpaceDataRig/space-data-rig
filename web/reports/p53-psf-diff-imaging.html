Reading (honest)

On 16 confirmed planets the PSF-model double-difference vetoed 1 at 2.0", down from 6 for the naive difference-image centroid (P44) on the SAME cubes -- a large reduction, so the PSF-gradient nuisance plus the displaced-PSF deltaChi2 gate genuinely tames the failure mode that made the naive centroid unsafe (coherent pointing / focus structure masquerading as an off-axis source). Still NOT zero false-kills (1 killed); one confirmed planet wrongly vetoed disqualifies the gate (measure-before-gate): the PSF method gets much closer than naive but is not yet safe to gate.

Veto power is unproven regardless. Every target here is a confirmed planet (all truly on-target), so a veto that rarely fires shows safety but NOT the ability to CATCH a real blend -- that needs a labelled negative class, which public token-free data cannot supply (see scripts/p52). The point-source drop is also faint on single-sector data, so the judge stays inconclusive on most targets (low amp snr).
