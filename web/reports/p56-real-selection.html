0/18 hosts have a measurable contaminant (dilution < 0.99). Even the most-inflated host (TOI-2128, dilution 0.996) raises the true 50% depth only from 300 to 301 ppm -- negligible. The honest finding is that this BRIGHT confirmed-planet sample is dilution-clean (neighbour counts reach into the teens but the companions are faint, so the flux-weighted dilution stays ~1); the selection function here is dominated by the geometric transit probability (which spans 0.13-0.39 across real stellar radii/masses) and by completeness. The dilution window would bite in crowded / faint fields, which the archive's confirmed sample under-represents.

This is a measured selection function, not an occurrence rate. P47 hosts are bright planet-bearing stars (this is detector efficiency, not an unbiased survey selection), and there are no detections to divide by.
