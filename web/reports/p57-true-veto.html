Honest caveat (why a modest power number here is NOT the whole story)

The FP/EB label does not distinguish an off-target blend (light from a neighbouring star -- exactly what a localization veto can catch) from an on-target eclipsing binary (the dip really is at the target -- which localization legitimately cannot reject and should not try; that needs a shape / odd-even discriminator). So the catch-rate above is a lower bound for the localization tool, not its true sensitivity. The precise, disqualifying number is the false-veto rate: a known planet wrongly killed. Whatever the power number, we do NOT gate on it here.
