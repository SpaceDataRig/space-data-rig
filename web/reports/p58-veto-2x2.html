P58 a larger, phase-refined true-veto 2x2 (priority-0 scale-up)

Reproduce: npm run p58:veto-2x2 -- --neg 24 --pos 24 --tmag 11 --boot 80 --phases 16 Same token-free ExoFOP labels as P57 (KP = keep, FP/EB = veto), but with more cases and a transit-phase refinement: the fold is modulo the period, so we sweep one full period and localize at the phase of the strongest real dip. This removes catalog-epoch mis-timing -- the likely cause of P57's single false kill -- as a confound.

Usable labelled cases: 36 (16 negatives, 20 positives) out of 48 sampled; 12 had no SPOC cube or no coherent transit.

Honest reading

Even with the timing confound removed, the FP/EB negative class still contains on-target eclipsing binaries that a localization veto legitimately cannot reject, so the catch-rate is a lower bound, not the tool's sensitivity. The disqualifying number is the false-veto rate: any known planet the refined PSF still kills is a genuine localization failure, and one is enough to keep the gate closed.
