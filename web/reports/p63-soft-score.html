P63 soft blend-likelihood score (continuous, not a veto) and its published false-kill rate

Reproduce: npm run p63:soft-score (pure offline re-derivation from reports/p61-llm-hypothesis.json; adds no new measurement). P57-P59 showed the pixel/dilution tools false-kill known planets, so they are turned into a continuous 0..1 blend-likelihood (src/candidate/softScore.ts) that the critic may use ONLY as a bounded ranking weight -- never a gate.

On 28 labelled targets (16 confirmed planets, 12 labelled blends) with a determined continuous signal: mean blend-likelihood 0.39 (planets) vs 0.50 (blends).
