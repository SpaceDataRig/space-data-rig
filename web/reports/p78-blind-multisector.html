P78 multi-sector blind test on real photometry

Reproduce: npm run p78:blind-multisector -- --stars 24 --sectors 4 --inject-frac 0.5 --seed 11

Where P49 stitched sectors into one baseline and forced corroboratedSectors=1 (so the 2-sector bar accepted nothing by construction), P78 runs the detector SECTOR BY SECTOR on real TESS-SPOC curves, plants one phase-coherent ephemeris across the sectors (or leaves real nulls), and counts corroboration as the number of independent sectors that found the same period. The 2-sector bar is then MEASURED, not assumed.

24 hosts, all with >=2 real sectors (10 injected, 14 null), 55 period-clusters

Honest reading: precision is a LOWER bound (a detection that is the host's own real planet counts FP). The decisive results are whether injected planets clear the 2-sector bar (accepted recall) and whether null hosts stay at zero false accepts under it -- i.e. whether real multi-sector corroboration suppresses real noise.
