P92 scrambled false-alarm null and a data-driven completeness/reliability gate

Reproduce: npm run p92:scrambled-fa -- --targets 10 --perCurve 3 --maxsectors 1 --seed 7

LEO-Vetter (Kunimoto et al. 2025, ApJ) tunes its flux-level vetting by minimising sqrt((1-C)^2 + (1-R_FA)^2), completeness on injected planets and reliability against false alarms drawn from a time-SCRAMBLED population -- and it shows scrambling is the honest false-alarm surrogate where INVERSION is not (orbit-start scattered-light ramps are asymmetric under reflection; flares invert into dips). This repo had a per-candidate phase-scrambling FAP and an inversion null but no candidate-level false-alarm POPULATION scored jointly with completeness. On 10 real TESS-SPOC curves we build exactly that: 50 injected transits, 57 scrambled TCEs (shuffle + block), 0 inverted TCEs, all through src/detect/falseAlarmNull.ts.
