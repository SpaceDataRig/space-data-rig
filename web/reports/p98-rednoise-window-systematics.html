3. Window function (real curves): daily sidelobe / alias ambiguity at the injected period

Median daily-window power on the real curves: 0.011 (TESS cadence gaps sit near the daily frequency, so this is the residual leakage the roadmap flagged -- and, as P43 showed, it is small: the daily window is largely CLOSED, so window aliasing is NOT the binding wall here). Highest alias ambiguity: 0.77 (ratio of the best +/-1 c/d sidelobe SNR to the peak).

4. Sector / instrument drift: O-C coherence (real curves)

12/12 real curves timed a coherent O-C line at the injected period; median coherence 0.54 (an aggregate inconsistency penalty: a signal that does not time to one linear ephemeris across the baseline scores low and is penalised in the joint ranking). Coherence is HIGH on the single-sector curves (HD 39091 0.97, HR 858 0.96, GJ 143 / HD 1397 0.93) and LOW on the long STITCHED multi-season baselines (AU Mic, HD 25463, TOI-480 ~0.02) -- exactly the P12/P29 finding that a fixed period drifts out of phase over a 700-day stitched baseline, so sector drift is only measurable with the per-sector ephemeris product, not a coarse single fold. Stated as a ceiling, not a lever this study pays.

Reading (honest)

Modeling the noise color, not resampling it, is the precision lever: at rho>=0.3 the model-free block surrogate fires 30.0% false alarms while the AR(1) MODEL null holds 0.0%, near nominal 5%, lifting per-candidate precision from ~77% to ~100% at matched recall. On the real corpus the model false-rejects 0 of 12 unambiguous injections -- it cuts FAR without dropping true planets. This QUANTIFIES the part of the precision wall that systematics modeling can pay back; it does not remove the wall: coherent instrumental systematics shared across stars (P31's common-mode class) and the faint crowded blend regime (P85, axis 1) are separate, and the window/coherence levers above are shown to be small-or-undetermined here.
